Counter-before-second-hash model for the secp*k1 high material (§4.2(b))
========================================================================
Stage 1 gives the shared V; the counter lives only in stage 2, so V stays
shared and §7.3.8 does not apply. Stage 2 operates on the digest V (known),
so the model is testable. Targets (high 32-bit limb above V):
    0x0000003b  secp224k1/256k1 high limb
    0x0554123b  secp192k1 high limb

A. Plain transforms of V (no counter): CRC32, Adler32, word sum/xor, slices
    checked 3 forms x 7 transforms; 0 hit(s) so far.

B. Windows of H2(V) with no counter (is the limb a slice of a re-hash?)
    324 window checks over 9 hashes; 0 limb hit(s).

C. Counter before the second hash: windows of H2(enc(V, i)), small i
    25,559,040 window checks (fast hashes to i<65536, slow to i<4096).
    0x0000003b (secp224k1/256k1 high limb): no small-counter second-hash window produces it.
    0x0554123b (secp192k1 high limb): no small-counter second-hash window produces it.
    Joint recipe (one hash+enc+reading yielding both 0x0000003b and 0x0554123b):
      none: no single second-hash recipe reproduces both limbs at small i.

D. Limits of this test
    * If stage 2 hashes the SEED (not the digest), its input is unknown and
      this cannot test it; only the digest-input form is checkable.
    * The limb is 6 bits (224/256) or 28-32 bits (192). A 6-bit target is
      met by ~1/64 of counters, so a 0x3b match alone means nothing; a 32-bit
      match (0x0000003b, 0x0554123b) at a SMALL counter would be meaningful.
    * A large counter could match any 32-bit target by luck (~1 per 2^32); a
      bounded sweep tests the small-counter regime a real recipe would use,
      not impossibility.
    * Step inversion (scripts/sha1_steps.py) needs a full 160-bit target to
      run backwards; we observe only a 6-32 bit limb, so it does not apply.

CONCLUSION
  No plain transform of V, no window of H2(V), and no small-counter second
  hash H2(V||i) reproduces the 32-bit high limbs 0x0000003b / 0x0554123b,
  and no single recipe yields both. The counter-before-second-hash variant
  is not supported in the digest-input, small-counter regime. With §7.3.8
  (counter in stage 1) already excluded, the high material still reads as a
  hand-assigned tag (§7.3.7), not a computed one. Only the seed-input form
  of stage 2 remains beyond reach.
